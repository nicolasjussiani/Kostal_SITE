# KOSTAL Brasil 3D Experience

## Design read
For automotive engineers, distributors, repair professionals, and fleet buyers who need to feel precision before reading specifications.

## Concept spine
The site is a signal path: energy enters a dark technical chamber, passes through controls and connectors, and resolves as reliable motion.

## Delivery tier
Spectacle: a scroll-controlled cinematic opening, followed by a real WebGL product lab using the supplied GLB models.

## Locked palette
- Night navy: #061426
- Deep blue: #173B65
- Steel: #A8B5C4
- Signal orange: #E87722
- White: #F6F8FB
The blue and orange family follows KOSTAL's existing brand identity, so the usual dark-orange avoidance is intentionally overridden. Orange is the only accent.

## Locked type
Satoshi for display and body with JetBrains Mono for technical labels. Both reinforce modern automotive engineering without editorial ornament.

Animation mode: animated-website

## Journey block
Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous 15-second camera move through an exploded steering-column assembly keeps one world and avoids seams.
Journey:
1. Entrada: a steering control emerges from darkness. Headline: "Precisão em cada comando." Body: "Componentes eletromecânicos projetados para responder com confiança."
2. Interface: the camera reveals tactile controls. Headline: "Controle que se sente." Body: "Ergonomia, resistência e resposta reunidas no ponto de contato."
3. Sinal: a piezo buzzer and connector align along a pulse of light. Headline: "Sinais sem ruído." Body: "Conexões consistentes para ambientes automotivos exigentes."
4. Movimento: the clock spring rotates into place. Headline: "Movimento contínuo." Body: "Energia e informação atravessam o volante com liberdade."
5. Acesso: the key fob closes the assembly. Headline: "Tecnologia que acompanha." Body: "Soluções integradas do acesso à condução."
World grammar: dark navy studio, anodized metal and matte polymer, KOSTAL orange only as signal light, cool key light from upper left, soft blue rim light from rear right, shallow haze, precise reflections, no text in imagery.
Mobile framing: every focal object remains within the center 60 percent; copy occupies a lower safe band and mobile video is capped at 720px.
Delivery budget: desktop film at or below 32 MiB; mobile film at or below 16 MiB.
The journey enacts the signal-path concept by turning five components into one continuous electrical and mechanical system.

## Interaction principles
The scroll controls the film; pointer drag controls the WebGL model. Motion communicates assembly, continuity, and response.
Reduced motion shows the exact poster, all chapter copy, and a static model image without fetching video.
Corners are sharp with clipped technical notches; no pill buttons.

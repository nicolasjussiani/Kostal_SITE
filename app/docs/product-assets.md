## Provenance and limitations

Existing Tripo models were matched visually against the official first-product photographs. The former window-switch model depicts the Remate; steering-control depicts the roof light; piezo-buzzer depicts the ignition switch; electrical-connector depicts the warning-light socket. Model names from automatic generation were not reliable product metadata.

The window-module model was exported without a new paid generation from Tripo project 58ae39cd-d663-4660-9694-a0d7349d6ed2. Its 5,420,704-byte GLB was optimized to 194,472 bytes using Meshopt and WebP 1024, preserving geometry.

Eight existing models are currently wired in. The emergency button 12057061 is the only identified product without a matching model. Its official photograph is shown, explicitly labeled as not yet available in 3D. The Tripo image-to-model dry run succeeded, but generation requires account authentication and confirmation of approximately 30 credits. No paid generation was submitted during this catalogue correction.

Models are illustrative reconstructions, not dimensional CAD or compatibility guarantees. Product application links target the exact official SKU. Category/source selection is a September 2026 snapshot rather than a live catalogue integration.

## Validation

TypeScript and production build passed. Local asset existence, unique category/SKU mapping, and binary GLB decoding are checked before deploy. The viewer loads only the selected model and retains rotate, zoom, view presets, grid, wireframe and full-screen controls.
